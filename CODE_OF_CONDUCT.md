# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Telegram Bot 2 to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give feedback that is specific, useful, and focused on the work.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy; do not publish personal information or private conversations.
- Welcome questions from beginners and explain technical decisions clearly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, impersonation, or deliberately misleading project reports.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or contributor workflows.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content and may restrict participation when necessary. To report an issue, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub.
